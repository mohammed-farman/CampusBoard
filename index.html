Progress & study rewards

Tasks earn 10 XP (up to 5 task rewards a day), assignments 20 XP, a manually completed 5-minute focus block 15 XP, and each daily quest 20 XP once. Achievements award 10 XP once. Levels use fixed personal XP thresholds. Streaks count distinct local dates with genuine completed study actions. Tasks and study progress remain in this browser; Firebase currently stores your account and profile only.

Student profile

This profile powers your local posts, people suggestions, and study groups. When signed in, your display name, interests, and profile images can also sync through Firebase.
